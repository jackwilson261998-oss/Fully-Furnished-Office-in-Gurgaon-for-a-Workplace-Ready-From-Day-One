What to Check Before You Move Your Team
A workplace can look impressive during a quick visit and still fail to meet a company's actual requirements. Before committing, businesses should examine how the office will function on an ordinary working day. They should also understand exactly what is included in the commercial package rather than assuming every visible facility is automatically part of the quoted cost.
Furniture and seating: Check the condition, comfort, quantity, and arrangement of desks, chairs, storage, and other furniture.
Connectivity: Confirm internet availability, network infrastructure, and communication requirements.
Meeting requirements: Make sure meeting rooms or discussion areas are adequate for internal and client-facing work.
Daily operations: Clarify housekeeping, maintenance, security, power backup, and related support services.
Commercial terms: Understand rent, deposit, tenure, taxes, utilities, and additional charges.
Team capacity: Confirm whether the available configuration suits the current workforce.
Future expansion: Ask whether more seats, cabins, or workspace can be added when the business grows.
Physical inspection: Visit the office before signing so the actual environment can be assessed.
